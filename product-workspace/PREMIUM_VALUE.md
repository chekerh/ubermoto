## Premium value — why users pay

### Who pays
Primary payer is the **merchant** (B2B): UberMoto is their delivery + growth channel. Customers may remain free; drivers may have optional upsells later.

### What merchants pay for (clear before/after)

- **More sales, less chaos**
  - Branded catalog and product management
  - Promotions and campaigns that actually move revenue
  - Operational controls (availability windows, delivery radius, surge participation)

- **Operational certainty**
  - Reliable dispatch + tracking reduces customer support load
  - Refund/support tooling and ticketing reduces time spent on WhatsApp/phone
  - Analytics: what sells, peak times, conversion from promo campaigns

- **Trust + compliance**
  - Verified driver program increases customer trust
  - Safer handling of uploads (docs) and auditability for admin decisions

### Why not a free workaround
- WhatsApp orders + random couriers lack tracking, accountability, consistent pricing, and analytics.
- Generic ecommerce tools don’t solve last-mile dispatch + driver workflows.

### Retention mechanics (recurring value)
- Order history + reorder
- Subscription plan unlocks ongoing campaigns + analytics
- Admin-managed announcements, seasonal promotions, and configurable pricing
- Driver reliability + verification improves as platform grows

### Tiering philosophy
- Free tier must be useful, but paid tiers must remove meaningful friction and add measurable ROI (not artificial locks).

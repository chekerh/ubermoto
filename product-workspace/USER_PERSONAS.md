## User personas

### 1) “Amina” — busy customer (Tunis)
- **Goals**: get delivery fast; know ETA; pay fair price.
- **Frustrations**: “Where is my driver?”, surprise fees, unreliable couriers.
- **Motivations**: convenience, safety, predictability.
- **How we serve**: realtime tracking, clear statuses, saved addresses, reorder, transparent delivery fee + surge disclosure.

### 2) “Hichem” — merchant owner (small grocery / pharmacy)
- **Goals**: increase sales, reduce cancellations, avoid delivery coordination overhead.
- **Frustrations**: driver no-shows, customer complaints, manual promo management.
- **Motivations**: revenue growth + time savings.
- **How we serve**: paid merchant dashboard, campaign tools, operational settings, analytics, support tooling.

### 3) “Khaled” — driver (motorcycle courier)
- **Goals**: steady jobs, predictable payouts, less dispute risk.
- **Frustrations**: unsafe pickups, unclear addresses, delayed payments.
- **Motivations**: income, reputation, safety.
- **How we serve**: driver verification, job lifecycle clarity, navigation assistance, payout records, ratings.

### 4) “Sara” — operations/support agent (internal)
- **Goals**: resolve tickets quickly; keep fraud/abuse low; keep platform healthy.
- **Frustrations**: missing audit trails, unclear ownership, no admin tooling.
- **How we serve**: role-based admin dashboard, review queues (drivers/documents), audit logs, dynamic content/settings control.
